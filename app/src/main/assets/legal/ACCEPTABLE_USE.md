# IRA Acceptable Use Policy

**App:** IRA  
**Effective date:** 28 October 2026  
**Last updated:** 28 October 2026  
**Version:** 1.0-draft

This Acceptable Use Policy is part of the IRA Terms of Service. It explains what you may not do with IRA. If you do not agree, do not use IRA.

## 1. General rule

Use IRA only on devices you own or control, or on devices where you have clear permission from the owner. Use it only for lawful purposes and only in ways that respect the privacy, safety, and rights of other people.

## 2. Prohibited uses

You may not use IRA to:

- break any law, regulation, court order, or contractual obligation;
- surveil, monitor, stalk, track, or spy on another person;
- access another person's device, screen, notifications, contacts, messages, calls, accounts, or location without their informed consent;
- record or transcribe a conversation without the consent required by law;
- harass, threaten, intimidate, defame, or abuse anyone;
- send hate speech, slurs, or content that incites violence;
- impersonate another person or misrepresent your identity or affiliation;
- steal credentials, passwords, one-time codes, session tokens, or authentication factors;
- bypass security controls, parental controls, device administration, screen locks, Play Protect, or app permissions;
- install, distribute, or operate malware, spyware, stalkerware, or other harmful software;
- send spam, bulk messages, automated robocalls, or unsolicited marketing;
- commit fraud, scams, phishing, or financial theft;
- automate payments, money transfers, or purchases without explicit human review;
- violate the terms of WhatsApp, your carrier, your messaging app, or any other service;
- interact with emergency services, or rely on IRA in an emergency;
- make medical, legal, financial, or safety decisions without qualified human review;
- operate a vehicle, machinery, or other dangerous system in a way that violates safety law;
- harm minors, or expose minors to inappropriate content or contact;
- collect or process another person's personal data without a lawful basis and required consent;
- circumvent rate limits, access controls, or usage restrictions of a third-party service;
- reverse engineer or redistribute IRA in violation of the Terms;
- encourage or help anyone else do any of the above.

## 3. Sensitive permissions

IRA can request microphone, contacts, phone, SMS, accessibility, notification access, overlay, and system settings access. These permissions are powerful.

You must not:

- enable Accessibility or Notification Access on a device you do not own or control without permission;
- use IRA to read another person's notifications or screen without their consent;
- use IRA to send messages or place calls from another person's phone without their consent;
- grant IRA access to a shared device in a way that exposes another person's private data;
- use IRA to monitor an employee, partner, family member, or child without the consent and legal authority required in your jurisdiction.

## 4. No emergency or high-risk use

IRA is not an emergency service. Do not use it to call emergency numbers, send emergency messages, summon help, monitor a medical condition, operate a vehicle, control machinery, manage medication, or make decisions where a failure could cause death, injury, or serious financial loss.

## 5. Third-party services

When you use cloud chat, Gemini, Pollinations, DuckDuckGo, WhatsApp, your carrier, or another third-party service through IRA, you must follow that service's terms and policies. Do not use IRA to bypass their restrictions or to send prohibited content.

## 6. Security

Do not attempt to:

- probe, scan, or test the security of IRA or its providers without authorization;
- interfere with IRA's operation or another user's use;
- introduce malicious code or content;
- extract API keys, tokens, or secrets from the app or another device;
- use IRA to attack, overload, or disrupt a service.

If you find a security issue, report it privately to [security contact email]. Do not exploit it or disclose it publicly before we have had a reasonable chance to respond.

We may suspend or terminate your license, refuse support, remove access to a distribution channel, or report conduct to authorities if we believe you have violated this policy or the Terms. We may also act to protect users, third parties, or the public.

Uninstalling IRA does not cancel obligations that arose before you stopped using it, including obligations related to unlawful conduct.

## 8. Reporting abuse

Report abuse, impersonation, or unsafe use to [abuse contact email]. Include enough detail for us to understand the issue. Do not send sensitive personal data unless it is necessary.

We may update this policy. The updated version will show a new effective date. Continued use after the effective date means you accept the updated policy.
